About the Club

Cultivating Technical Excellence & Ethics

The Blackout College Technology Club was founded to create an environment where classroom computer science concepts transform into operational software and resilient network defense architectures.

We operate student-managed computing resources, organize regular technical hackathons, and champion open-source contributions. Our members gain direct experience debugging real-world systems, performing responsible security vulnerability research, and collaborating in multi-disciplinary teams.

Our Mission

"To give students practical experience in technology and cybersecurity."

Why Blackout?

  • Zero Prior Barrier

    From complete novices to seasoned coders, all students are welcomed with clear mentorship tracks.

  • Isolated Sandbox Labs

    Safely conduct penetration testing, network sniffing, and defensive hardening in segmented networks.

  • Alumni & Industry Ties

    Regular guest seminars with cybersecurity professionals and software architects.

Principles

Our Guiding Values

Integrity, ethical responsibility, and technical dedication govern everything we build and study.

Curiosity

We encourage asking how systems function under the hood, exploring packet flows, and deconstructing software to understand every layer.

Collaboration

Great engineering is never a solo pursuit. We foster open knowledge sharing, peer review, pair programming, and inclusive teamwork.

Responsibility

With offensive security skills comes ethical responsibility. We enforce a strict ethical pledge and prioritize defensive protection.

Continuous Learning

Technology evolves daily. We maintain perpetual curiosity, actively learning new languages, tools, and defensive frameworks.

Milestones

Our Growth Timeline

A look at our journey from a small informal study group to an active campus technology organization.

2023

Club Founded

Established by a cohort of 15 computer science students seeking hands-on systems programming and networking experience outside standard classroom hours.

2024

First Technology Showcase

Hosted the inaugural campus-wide software exhibition featuring 12 student projects, including early prototypes of the Campus Network Monitor and automated lab reservation tools.

2025

Cybersecurity Initiative Launched

Inaugurated the dedicated cybersecurity division with dedicated server hardware, conducting weekly capture-the-flag drills, secure coding workshops, and threat modeling tutorials.

2026

Prambh 2nd Year CTF Introduced

Formally established the "Prambh 2nd Year" collegiate capture the flag tournament platform, inviting student teams to test defensive and analytical cybersecurity skills.
