About Us

A Community Driven by Technical Curiosity

Founded by passionate engineering students, the Blackout College Technology Club serves as an incubator for practical computing skills, open-source innovation, and collaborative engineering.

Bridging Theory and Practice

While lecture halls deliver the foundational mathematics and theory, Blackout provides the sandboxes, server infrastructure, and mentorship required to build hardened production-grade applications and conduct security analysis.

Read Our Story →

Student-Led Collaboration

From peer-led code audits to weekly lab drills, our members work across cross-functional squads to develop campus tools, conduct defensive audits, and host regional collegiate computing contests.
